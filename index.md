# Privacy Policy – Caption Review

**Last updated:** November 11, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Caption Review is a Chrome browser extension designed to assist users with reviewing caption segments on the Encord platform.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the extension accesses, how that information is used, and how it is handled.

## 2. Information Accessed

Caption Review operates on supported Encord pages and may access information displayed in the Encord review interface, including:

- Caption segment information.
- Segment numbers and frame ranges.
- Review checklist selections made by the user.

This information is used solely to provide the extension's review and feedback functionality.

## 3. Data Collection and Sharing

Caption Review does not require users to create an account or provide personal information to use the extension.

The extension does not sell, rent, or share user information with advertisers or other third parties.

## 4. Data Storage

Review selections may be stored locally within the user's browser to support the extension's functionality.

Caption Review does not operate its own external database for storing review information.

## 5. Third-Party Services

Caption Review does not use third-party analytics, advertising services, or external caption-processing APIs.


## 6. Permissions

The extension requests access to supported Encord pages so it can display its review panel, identify caption segments, and provide review feedback.

Permissions are used only for the extension's stated functionality.

## 7. Data Security

The extension is designed to minimise access to information and use it only for its intended review functionality.

Users should keep their browser and Encord accounts secure.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the extension's functionality or data practices change. Updates will be published at the same Privacy Policy URL.
